01 / Abstract

Stable 3D understanding in a moving world.

Static-scene assumptions cause severe spatial feature misalignment when feed-forward 3D models encounter transient motion.

We propose SPAR, a joint semantic-geometric encoding architecture that explicitly isolates transient dynamic noise before latent space aggregation. A dynamic-region-aware end-to-end training paradigm structurally couples motion estimation with multi-view visual and semantic learning, enabling the network to distill temporally stable scene representations from dynamic inputs.

On D-RE10K, SPAR achieves state-of-the-art novel-view synthesis quality and strong self-supervised motion-mask prediction. The joint objective also reveals a useful inter-task synergy: semantic synthesis consistently improves photometric fidelity.

01

Cross-view motion

02

Joint scene tokens

Dynamic regions are suppressed before photometric and semantic evidence is aggregated into a compact scene representation.

03
